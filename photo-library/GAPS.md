## Images with uncertain licensing

None. Every candidate has a licence recorded by its source (Wikimedia Commons file page, Flickr via Openverse, iNaturalist photo record or Bugwood image record). Points to double-check:

- **Bugwood / IPM Images**: the licence is set per image by the contributor (CC BY 3.0 US or CC BY-NC 3.0 US) and is shown on each image page; Bugwood asks for the credit line "Photographer, Organisation, Bugwood.org".
- **iNaturalist**: licence is per photo; the photo page is recorded as `page` and the observation as `observation`.
- **Flickr via Openverse**: licence as indexed by Openverse; confirm on the Flickr page before release, as owners can change licences later (earlier copies stay licensed).
- **Wikimedia Commons**: a few files are Flickr or government mirrors; the Commons page states the licence review.
