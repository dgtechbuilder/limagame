# Photo credits

Every photo in the game is a real photograph under a licence that allows commercial use and adaptation (public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA).
Sources: Wikimedia Commons, Flickr (via Openverse), iNaturalist and Bugwood / IPM Images. The files here are cropped to 10:7, resized to
1000 × 700 and compressed; photos under a ShareAlike (SA) licence are shared under the same licence as their original. The game shows the
author and licence on each photo when it is zoomed in or shown as a clue. The full candidate library, with alternatives and review notes,
is in `photo-library/` (see `photo-library/manifest.json`).

`flood/farm`, `livestock/farm` and `wildlife/farm` are crop-agnostic scenes used for crops that have no photo of their own;
`_normal/soil` and `_normal/water` are neutral photos used where the soil or the water is not the problem.
