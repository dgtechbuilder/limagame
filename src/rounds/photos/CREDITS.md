# Photo credits

Every photo in the game comes from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) under a free licence.
The files here are cropped, resized and compressed versions of the originals. Photos under a ShareAlike (SA) licence are
shared under the same licence as their original. The game shows the author and licence on each photo when it is zoomed in
or shown as a clue.

`_normal/soil` and `_normal/water` are neutral photos (healthy soil; a full garden tank with drip lines), used for that picture in
situations where the soil or the water is not the problem.
